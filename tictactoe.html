How to play Tic Tac Toe

Players take turns placing X and O on a 3x3 grid. The first player to line up three of their own marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins the round. If all nine squares fill up with no line completed, the round is a draw.

2 Player or vs Computer

Switch to "2 Players" to pass the device back and forth with a friend, or pick "vs Computer" to play solo — the computer plays as O and always makes a valid, competitive move.
