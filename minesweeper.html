How to play Minesweeper

Tap a cell to reveal it. If it's a mine, the round ends — but your very first tap is always guaranteed safe, so you'll never lose on move one. A revealed number tells you exactly how many mines are hidden in the up-to-eight cells touching it. Use that information to work out which nearby cells are safe and which hide mines.

Flagging and winning

Right-click on desktop, or turn on Flag Mode and tap on mobile, to mark a cell you're sure hides a mine — this stops you from accidentally revealing it and helps you keep track. You win by revealing every single safe cell; you don't need to flag every mine to win.

Tips to get better at Minesweeper

Start near the middle of the board, since your first click is always guaranteed safe and tends to open up the most space. Use the numbers logically: if a numbered cell's count exactly matches its number of unrevealed neighbours, all of those neighbours are mines. If a numbered cell's count matches the number of already-flagged neighbours, every other neighbour is safe to reveal.

Frequently asked questions

Is the first click really always safe? Yes — mines are placed only after your first click, and never on or directly next to that first cell.

How do I flag a mine on mobile? Turn on Flag Mode with the toolbar button, then tap any cell to flag or unflag it.

What's the difference between difficulty levels? Easy is an 8x8 grid with 10 mines, Medium is 10x10 with 20 mines, and Hard is 12x12 with 30 mines.
