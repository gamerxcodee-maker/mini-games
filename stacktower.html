How to play Stack Tower

A block slides back and forth above your tower. Tap, click, or press Space to drop it onto the block below. Only the overlapping part sticks — anything hanging over the edge falls away, so the tower gets narrower with every imperfect drop. Miss completely with no overlap at all, and the run ends.

Perfect stacks and combos

Drop a block close enough to a perfect line-up and it snaps to the exact width of the one below instead of shrinking, and builds a combo streak worth bonus points. The tower speeds up the higher you go, so a long perfect streak becomes harder to keep alive the further you push it.
