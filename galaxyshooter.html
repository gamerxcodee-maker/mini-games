How to play Galaxy Shooter

Drag anywhere on the screen to move your ship left and right — it fires automatically, so all you need to focus on is positioning. Enemies drift down from the top in different patterns: some fly straight down, some weave side to side, and some fire back at you. Colliding with an enemy or getting hit by enemy fire costs a life. Collect the glowing power-ups that drop from destroyed enemies for temporary boosts.

Tips for surviving longer

Stay near the center of the screen by default so you have room to dodge in either direction — getting pinned against an edge limits your options. Prioritise destroying the shooting enemies first, since they're the main source of incoming fire, even if a weaving enemy looks like an easier target. Grab shield power-ups the moment you see them; they're the most valuable pickup when the screen gets crowded.

Frequently asked questions

What do the power-ups do? Rapid Fire speeds up your shots, Multi-Shot fires three bullets at once, and Shield makes you briefly invincible — each lasts a limited time.

Does the game get harder the longer I survive? Yes — enemies spawn faster and more of them shoot back as your score climbs.

How many lives do I get? Three lives per run; losing all three ends the game and shows your final score.
