How to play Chess

Standard chess rules apply. Tap a piece to see its legal moves highlighted, then tap a destination square to move. Castling, en passant, and pawn promotion are all supported — when a pawn reaches the far row, you'll be asked which piece to promote it to.

Playing with a friend online

Pick "Play with Friend", create a room, and share the code or invite link. Whoever creates the room plays White and moves first; whoever joins plays Black. Moves sync instantly over a direct connection between your two browsers.
