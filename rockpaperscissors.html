How to play Rock Paper Scissors

Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock. Pick your move each round — first to win 3 rounds takes the match. Play against the computer for quick practice, pass the device to a friend for local rounds, or connect online to play someone on a different device in real time.

Playing with a friend online

Pick "Play with Friend", create a room, and send the code or invite link to whoever you want to play. Both players pick a move each round — choices stay hidden until you've both locked one in, then they reveal together, just like playing in person.
