How to play Turbo Dash

Your car runs forward automatically and never stops. Use the left and right arrow keys (or A and D) on desktop, or tap the left and right side of the screen on mobile, to switch between three lanes. Barriers scroll toward you in some lanes while at least one lane stays open — pick it in time. Coins appear in the open lane as a reward for dodging correctly.

Scoring and speed

Your score climbs automatically the longer you survive, with bonus points for every coin collected and an extra reward for narrow near-misses. The road speeds up steadily and barrier patterns get tighter the longer you last, so the run always gets harder the deeper you go.
