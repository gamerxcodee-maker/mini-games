How to play 2048 Bright

Slide tiles with the arrow keys on desktop, or swipe with your finger on mobile. When two tiles carrying the same number touch, they merge into one tile with double the value. Keep merging to reach the 2048 tile — then keep going to see how high you can climb.

Why the colours are different

Most 2048 clones use the same brown-and-orange tile set. 2048 Bright uses a distinct, saturated colour for every tile value so you can read the board at a glance instead of squinting at similar shades.

Tips to get better at 2048

Keep your highest tile locked in one corner and avoid moving it away unless you have no other option. Build a chain of decreasing values along one edge so merges cascade predictably instead of scattering across the board. Try to leave one row or column open as a buffer to slide into, rather than filling the grid evenly on all sides.

Frequently asked questions

What's the highest tile possible? There's no hard cap — many players reach 4096, 8192 or higher by keeping their biggest tiles anchored to one corner.

Does the corner strategy really matter? Yes — it's the single most reliable technique, since it keeps your board organised and prevents your highest tiles from getting trapped in the middle.

Can I undo a move? Not currently — every swipe is final, so it helps to plan a move or two ahead before committing.
