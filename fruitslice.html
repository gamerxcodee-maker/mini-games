How to play Fruit Slice

Fruit launches up from the bottom of the screen in gentle arcs. Swipe your finger or mouse across a piece of fruit to slice it in half for points. Slice several fruits in one continuous swipe to trigger a combo bonus worth extra points. Letting three fruits fall unsliced ends the round — and slicing a bomb ends it instantly, no matter how many lives you have left.

Tips for bigger combos

Watch the pattern fruit launches in rather than reacting to each piece individually — after a few throws you can often predict where the next batch will peak and plan a single sweeping swipe through several at once. Long, fast swipes register combos more reliably than short taps, since the game checks your whole swipe path for fruit it crosses. When a bomb appears near a cluster of fruit, it's almost always safer to slice around it in a separate motion than to risk a combo swipe through the group.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I slice a bomb? The round ends immediately, regardless of how many lives or how high a score you have.

How are combos scored? Each fruit sliced within the same continuous swipe adds to that swipe's combo count, and the bonus grows with each additional fruit in the chain.

Does the game get faster over time? Yes — fruit launches more frequently and bombs appear more often the longer you survive.
