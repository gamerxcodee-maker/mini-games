How to play Line Drop

A piece made of four connected blocks falls from the top of the well. Move it left or right, rotate it, and either let it fall naturally or drop it faster. When an entire horizontal row is completely filled with blocks, that row clears and everything above shifts down.

Levels and scoring

Clearing multiple rows with a single piece scores far more than clearing them one at a time — a four-line clear is worth the most. Every ten lines you clear, the level goes up and pieces fall faster. The round ends when a new piece has nowhere to appear at the top of the well.

Tips to get better at Line Drop

Keep your stack as flat and even as possible rather than building tall spikes in one column — a flat surface makes it much easier to clear lines. Save the long straight piece for clearing multiple lines at once when you can, since a four-line clear scores far more than four separate single-line clears. Rotate pieces before they land rather than after — last-second rotations near the bottom can fail if there's no room.

Frequently asked questions

What are the seven piece shapes? The classic I, O, T, S, Z, J and L shapes, each made of four connected blocks.

How does the level system work? Every 10 lines cleared increases your level by one and speeds up how fast pieces fall.

What's the highest-scoring move? Clearing four lines at once with a single piece scores significantly more than clearing four lines one at a time.
