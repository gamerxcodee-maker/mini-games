Why play on Bright Arcade

Every game here is built the same way: fast to load, simple to pick up, and free to play for as long as you want, on desktop or mobile.

No installs, no accounts

There's nothing to download and nothing to sign up for. Open a game and you're playing within a second or two, even on a slow connection.

Works on any device

Every game is built to fit a phone screen just as well as a desktop monitor, with touch controls on mobile and keyboard controls on desktop.

Original where it counts

Alongside familiar formats like 2048 and Snake, Bright Arcade includes original ideas like Mirror Flap — games you won't find built quite the same way anywhere else.

New games over time

This is a growing collection. More games get added as they're finished, so it's worth checking back if you've cleared everything here.
