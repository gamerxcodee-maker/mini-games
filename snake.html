How to play Snake Bright

Guide the snake around the board to eat the pink food. Every piece of food eaten makes the snake grow by one segment and adds to your score. The game ends if the snake runs into the wall or into its own body, so plan your turns ahead of time.

Tips to get a higher score

Stick to the edges early on to keep the centre of the board open for later, when your snake is longer and needs more room to turn. Try to move in loops rather than straight lines across the whole board — it gives you more escape routes as the snake grows.
