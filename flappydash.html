How to play Flappy Dash

Tap or click inside the game board, or press Space, to make the bird flap upward. Gravity pulls it back down between flaps. Each pair of pipes you pass adds one point. Touching a pipe, the ground or the top edge ends the run. You can start without a download or account.

Controls, scoring and restarting

Use individual taps, mouse clicks or Space presses to flap; holding Space does not produce extra flaps. Try Again opens a fresh ready screen, then Start begins the next run. Your Best score is saved in this browser and remains when a run ends. Clearing the site’s browser data can remove that saved score.

Tips to get better at Flappy Dash

Use short, light taps rather than holding down — the bird responds to individual flaps, so over-tapping sends it too high. Try to keep a steady rhythm rather than reacting to each pipe separately; a consistent tap cadence is easier to control than last-second corrections. Aim to pass through the vertical center of each gap instead of hugging one edge, which gives you more room for error on the next pipe.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game get faster the longer I survive? No. This version uses a fixed pipe speed and gap size. The gap’s vertical position changes randomly, so each run still requires fresh timing.

What ends a run? Touching a pipe, the ground or the top edge ends the run immediately. A new-best message appears only when you exceed your previous best score.

Can I play with a keyboard? Yes — press Space to flap on desktop, or tap the screen on mobile.
