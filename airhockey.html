Air Hockey

Drag your mallet to hit the puck into your opponent's goal. First to 7 wins.

How to play Air Hockey

Drag your mallet anywhere within your half of the table to block and strike the puck. Hit it past your opponent's mallet and into their goal to score. First player to reach 7 goals wins the match. Play against the computer, share the screen with a friend for local two-player, or connect online with a friend on a different device.

Playing with a friend online

Pick "Play with Friend", create a room, and share the code or invite link. The player who creates the room defends the bottom goal; whoever joins defends the top. Puck movement is kept in sync between both browsers over a direct connection.

Tips to get better at Air Hockey

Stay closer to your own goal than the center line by default — it's easier to react to an incoming shot from a defensive position than to chase the puck back after committing forward. Strike the puck off-center when you want to change its angle sharply, similar to hitting a pool ball. Watch your opponent's mallet position, not just the puck itself — where they're set up to hit from tells you where to expect the puck next.

Frequently asked questions

How many goals win a match? First to 7 goals wins.

Can two people play on one phone? Yes — in 2 Players mode, both people can drag their own mallet at the same time using multi-touch.

Does the puck ever get stuck? No — it bounces off all four walls and resets automatically to the center after every goal.
