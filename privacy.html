Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Bright Arcade is a free browser-game website at brightarcade.in. This page explains the information used when you browse, play, contact us or choose optional services.

Playing and browser storage

No account is required. Games may store best scores, settings and progress in localStorage on your device. The homepage stores recently played games. Our privacy controls store your analytics choice. Clearing this site’s browser data removes these saved items.

Optional analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 only after you choose “Allow analytics” in our privacy controls. Until then, our shared analytics code does not load Google Analytics. If enabled, Google receives information such as page views, browser and device details, approximate location and interaction events; Google processes connection information including your IP address. Analytics may use cookies to recognise a browser across visits. Reports help us understand which pages visitors use.

You can change your choice using “Privacy choices” in the footer. Rejecting analytics stops future analytics loading and reloads the page; our script also attempts to remove accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies. You can clear remaining cookies in your browser. Read how Google uses information from partner sites.

Hosting, fonts and online games

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting providers receive requests and may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs. Pages request fonts from Google Fonts. Some games load PeerJS from a third-party CDN and use a signalling service when you create or join an online room. Multiplayer connections exchange game information with the other player and may reveal network information needed to establish the connection. When a direct connection is unavailable, Metered TURN servers can relay the encrypted game traffic and process connection information such as IP addresses and bandwidth usage. Avoid sharing private information in room codes or links.

Advertising

Advertising is planned; this version does not load Google AdSense ads. If Google advertising is enabled later, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this and other websites. Any required advertising consent controls must be configured before ads are enabled. Our optional analytics choice is not advertising consent.

You can manage personalised advertising in Google My Ad Center and learn about Google’s advertising technologies. This policy will be reviewed when advertising providers are enabled.

Contact and sharing

If you email gamerxcodee@gmail.com, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or sensitive information. Share links open third-party services only when you choose to use them. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Children and privacy requests

We do not require players to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information by email, or you want to ask about information you have sent us, contact gamerxcodee@gmail.com. Information held by Google or hosting providers is also subject to their own policies and controls.

Changes

We will update the revision date when this policy changes. Contact us through our contact page with questions.
