How to play Solitaire

Build each foundation pile up from Ace to King, one suit at a time. In the tableau, stack cards in descending order with alternating colours (a red 9 on a black 10, for example). Tap the stock pile to draw a card to the waste pile, then tap a card to select it and tap the pile you want to move it to. Only Kings can start a new pile in an empty tableau column.

Tips for winning more games

Uncover the tableau's face-down cards as early as possible rather than rushing cards to the foundations, since a card sent to the foundation too early can sometimes block a useful tableau move later. Always move an Ace or a 2 to the foundation the moment it's available — there's rarely a reason to hold one back. When you have a choice between two equally valid moves, prefer the one that flips a new face-down card over one that doesn't.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when the stock runs out? Tap the empty stock pile to recycle the waste pile back into the stock, face down, ready to draw again.

Can I move a card back from the foundation? No — once a card is placed on a foundation in this version, it stays there.

When does Auto-Complete become available? As soon as every tableau card is face up, the button activates and will automatically finish sending every card to its foundation.
