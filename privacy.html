Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Bright Arcade ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you visit and play games on this website. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts, sign-ups, or personal information to play any game on this site. Some games save your best score or progress locally in your browser's storage; this data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising networks. These providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites, in order to serve ads based on your interests.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also review the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page for other participating advertising networks.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site, such as which games are played and how often. This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Third-party links

This site includes share buttons for services such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Reddit, and Instagram. If you use these buttons, you'll be taken to or interact with that third party's platform, which has its own privacy policy and terms that we don't control.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
