How to play Word Streak

You have six tries to guess a hidden five-letter word. After each guess, each letter's tile changes colour: green means that letter is in the word and in the right spot, yellow means it's in the word but in the wrong spot, and grey means it isn't in the word at all. Use the on-screen keyboard, or just type and press Enter on desktop.

Daily vs Practice

Daily mode gives everyone the same word each day, and solving it keeps your streak alive — miss a day and the streak resets. Practice mode gives you a fresh random word every time you want to play, with no effect on your streak, so you can warm up or play as many rounds as you like.

Tips to get better at Word Streak

Start your first guess with a word that has several common vowels and consonants, like CRANE or ADIEU, to gather the most information early. Use grey (absent) letters to eliminate options rather than trying them again in later guesses. Pay close attention to yellow letters' position — a yellow letter means it's in the word but not in that exact spot, so try it in a different position next time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the daily word the same for everyone? Yes — the daily word is generated from the current date, so everyone playing Word Streak on the same day gets the same word.

What happens if I miss a day? Your streak resets to zero the next time you play if you don't solve that day's word.

Can I keep playing after solving the daily word? Yes — switch to Practice mode for unlimited random words that don't affect your streak.
